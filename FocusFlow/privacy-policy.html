Privacy Policy

FocusFlow is designed as a local-first productivity and planning tool. This policy explains the privacy principles for the website and browser extension.

Information Stored by FocusFlow

Tasks, projects, goals, routines, settings, and extension preferences are intended to be stored locally in your browser unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

No Data Selling

FocusFlow does not sell your personal information. The product is not built around advertising profiles or data resale.

Tracking

FocusFlow does not use tracking by default. If analytics or optional diagnostics are introduced later, they should be clearly disclosed and controlled by user settings.

Export and Backup

FocusFlow may provide export and backup tools so you can keep your planning data portable. Exported files are your responsibility once downloaded or moved outside the extension.

Website Links

The website links to third-party services such as GitHub, Patreon, and Buy Me A Coffee. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@liquidskysecurity.com.
